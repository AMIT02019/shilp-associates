How We Hire

Our 3-Step Selection Process

1. Application & Portfolio Review

Submit your resume and selected portfolio showcasing your design sensibility, technical drafting, or liaison/site coordination achievements.

2. Technical & Design Interaction

A focused discussion with our departmental leads on design methodology, software proficiency (AutoCAD, Revit, BIM), or regulatory DCR acumen.

3. Leadership Discussion & Offer

An interactive conversation with our senior leadership to align on career aspirations, role expectations, and mutual growth opportunities.
