Company Overview

Designing Spaces. Building Trust. Shaping Tomorrow.

Shilp Associates is a multidisciplinary firm offering Liaison, Project Management Consultancy (PMC), Architectural Design and Interior Design services across Mumbai and Pune. For over four decades we have partnered with housing societies, developers and institutions to carry projects from feasibility through statutory approvals, design, construction monitoring and handover.

Our practice is built on the belief that exceptional architecture is measured by the value it creates — for the people who live and work in it, and for the client who commissions it. We embed regulatory compliance and statutory approval strategy into the design process from day one, so projects move faster, cost less to build, and still deliver on design excellence.
