Step into the Legacy of Design Excellence with Shilp Associates

For over four decades, Shilp Associates has stood as a beacon of architectural innovation, due diligence, and structural mastery across Mumbai and Pune. Founded by Sandeep Patel, the firm continues to blend heritage with forward-thinking design, delivering over 500 iconic residential, commercial, PMC, and municipal liaison milestones.

At Shilp Associates, we don't just build structures—we bring visions to life. Whether you're envisioning a complex urban development requiring municipal liaison expertise or high-density housing with turnkey execution, our doors are always open to your aspirations.

Sandeep Patel
Founder & Principal Architect
