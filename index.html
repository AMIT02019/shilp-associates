Foreign Collaborations & Global Design Practice

Extending our architectural legacy beyond national borders, Shilp Associates actively collaborates on international master planning, cross-border architectural advisory, and global technology partnerships — infusing international design excellence into sustainable Indian redevelopment.

INTERNATIONAL MASTERPLANNING PROJECT

National Capital Region of the Philippines

Shilp Associates engaged in strategic architectural master planning and high-density urban infrastructure planning for the National Capital Region (NCR) of the Philippines — specifically the historically prominent, densely populated Tondo district in Metro Manila.

The project encompasses comprehensive socio-residential layout planning, transit-oriented development (TOD), and industrial-residential integration across one of Southeast Asia's most dynamic metropolitan sectors.
