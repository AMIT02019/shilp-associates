Service Overview

An Independent Advisor for Your Redevelopment

We provide comprehensive Project Management Consultancy (PMC) services for residential, redevelopment, commercial, and institutional projects. Our role is to coordinate and monitor the project from the initial planning stage through approvals, construction, and completion.

Our approach focuses on effective planning, coordination, quality control, cost management, timely execution, and transparent communication among the society, consultants, contractors, developers, authorities, and other stakeholders.

Our PMC Workflow

Four Phases, One Coordinated Process

Phase 1 — Feasibility & Society Advisory

Collecting society documents, licensed survey, DP remarks and NOCs, and preparing the project feasibility report for society approval.

Phase 2 — Developer Selection & Tender Management

Preparing the tender/offer invitation, comparative statements, site visits, and recommendations to help the society finalise a developer.

Phase 3 — Planning, Design & Regulatory Compliance

Coordinating agreements and drawings, reviewing developer plans, and working with the society to get plans approved by authorities.

Phase 4 — Construction Monitoring & Delivery

Periodic site supervision, quality control, regular society meetings, and coordination through to completion and occupation certificates.
